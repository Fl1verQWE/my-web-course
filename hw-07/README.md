# FRAGILE — Product Cards

FRAGILE is a modern e-commerce website featuring a collection of customizable product cards with a stylish black-and-purple design. The website includes a product gallery with images, NEW and discount labels, and a favorites feature. Its goal is to provide users with an attractive and convenient way to explore products.
